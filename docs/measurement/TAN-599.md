## Goal

## Constraints

Internal user/task/file/checkout keys never enter third-party analytics. Source absence is unknown unless a pre-registration first-party observation confirms an empty referrer/UTM. Such direct means browser-observed direct; stripped referrers cannot be distinguished. Historical aggregates cannot be backfilled into individual events. Authentication behavior belongs to TAN-597; do not edit auth implementation. Use additive tables, opt-in collection, isolated database and port. Analytics failure must not fail business actions.

## Scope

Capture a signed HttpOnly first-touch cookie through a same-origin endpoint before login, and bind it to the authenticated new account after return only when capture precedes account creation. Retain internal client-reported ready/export initiation, server-confirmed saved/committed delivery and owned checkout handoff records; validate every supplied business key against the authenticated owner. Do not call a download click a saved file. Browser cannot prove disk write; export initiation is a separate usage action. Query task acceptance from billing_tasks/ai_usage; signed provider records remain payment truth.

## Acceptance

Source/medium/referrer class and safe entry route survive a registration return; stale/missing/tampered/post-registration context stays unknown. Returning login never overwrites acquisition. Duplicate event IDs are idempotent. Cross-user keys and cross-origin requests are rejected. Ready/render failures, empty saves, failed exports and created-only checkouts do not count as successful use/handoff. Use rate counts distinct delivered result keys, with missing associations exposed. Exclude explicit QA IDs (including local samples) and separately disclose unclassified traffic. Fixed registration +24h and delivery +24h windows require maturity at the supplied as-of. Paywall uses each account's first exposure within the cohort window and same-user source=paywall handoff in the next 24h; orders and payment are reported separately.

## Attribution and privacy limits

The existing server Plausible event sets canonical `/auth/login` and does not supply original referrer/entry; OAuth return headers alone cannot recover the landing context. Counts 337 vs 337 establish numerical agreement only, not one-event/one-account identity. Internal first-party acquisition restores independently auditable account attribution; Plausible visitor attribution/entry may still remain Direct/None. No cross-platform identity field was added. The historical 272 visitors, 337 events/accounts, 103 download visitors/194 clicks and 1/66 node ratio remain historical observations; they cannot be rewritten into these internal rates.

`direct_observed` means the browser supplied empty referrer and no UTM source on the captured page, before account creation. Referrer policy/ad blockers/app browsers can strip source, so true navigation provenance is unknowable in that case. Same-origin referrer, invalid UTM, missing/expired/tampered token or capture after signup become unknown. First capture is a server-timed, signed HttpOnly cookie (30 days, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS); known prior capture is retained across auth/locale returns. Binding is immutable, token reuse across accounts is prevented by a unique capture key, and the cookie is consumed after successful bind. Capture blocked by JS/network, signup before capture completes, another device/browser, or a first landing through a simultaneous uncookied tab can leave unknown/last completed capture. This is an observation boundary, not deterministic advertising attribution. Acquisition cookies are not sent to third parties; safe entry routes remove private IDs and all query strings, referrers keep host only, UTM source/medium use bounded labels.
